For your setup:
nvidia-prime: likely not needed. PRIME is for switching or offloading between integrated and NVIDIA graphics. Your Xeon E5-1650 v3/HP Z440 setup is a desktop workstation, not a hybrid-graphics laptop.
nvidia-container-toolkit: only if Ollama runs inside Docker and needs GPU access. If Ollama runs directly on Arch and Open WebUI is the only container, Ollama uses the host NVIDIA driver; the Open WebUI container doesn’t need the toolkit just to connect to Ollama.
